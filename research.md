## Research
The article "Goal Setting and Student Achievement: A Longitudinal Study" examines a 5-year study exploring the relationship between goal setting and student achievement. Although this article focuses on goal setting in an academic setting and my assignment considers my 5-year goals across four broader, more personal categories, I can apply the same principles of self-efficacy, action planning, and reflection to achieve my goals, as the study suggests.
